Exercise with purpose.
Movement with direction.

Pain, an injury, surgery or a long-term condition can make everyday movement feel uncertain. Biokinetics is a healthcare profession that uses scientifically based exercise interventions to help you improve physical functioning, health and conditioning.

We assess your needs, develop an appropriate exercise plan and guide you through your movement journey.

What does a Biokineticist do?

Assess

We assess your physical status, movement, strength and physical capacity according to your needs and goals.

Prescribe

We design an individualised exercise programme that is appropriate to you.

Guide

We guide and educate you through your exercises with proper technique and understanding.

Progress

We monitor your progress and adapt your programme as your needs and goals change.

How Biokinetics can help

Biokinetics can form part of the management of a wide range of conditions and goals.

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Exercise-based rehabilitation and conditioning for individuals with cardiac conditions, within the scope and practice.

Respiratory Conditions

Exercise interventions to support respiratory function and physical conditioning.

Orthopaedic Rehabilitation

Exercise-based rehabilitation for injuries, post-surgery rehabilitation and musculoskeletal conditions.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Exercise interventions to assist with function, mobility and independence following neurological conditions.

Chronic Conditions

Exercise as part of a broader approach to managing chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension and others.

Strength & Conditioning

Building strength, endurance and physical capacity for everyday life, sport and performance goals.
