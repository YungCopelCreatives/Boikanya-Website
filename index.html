Neurological Recovery

Neuro-Plasticity & Functional Independence

Tailored movement therapy for individuals recovering from stroke, traumatic brain injuries, spinal cord injuries, Parkinson's Disease, and Multiple Sclerosis. We activate neuromuscular pathways to improve balance and gait.

  • Balance, proprioception & fall prevention training
  • Spasticity management and functional gait re-education
  • Repetitive task-oriented motor control drills

Maternal Movement Health

Safe Trimester Exercise & Postpartum Recovery

Specialized clinical support through all three trimesters and post-delivery recovery. We address pelvic girdle pain, diastasis recti resolution, pelvic floor integrity, and safe return to high-impact fitness.

  • Trimester-safe strength and postural adaptation
  • Diastasis recti screening & core re-engagement
  • Post-caesarean and vaginal delivery rehabilitation

Cultural Heritage in Motion

Indigenous Games as Movement Therapy

A groundbreaking clinical approach pioneering the therapeutic benefits of traditional African play—such as Diketo, Morabaraba, Kgati, and Dibeke—for pediatric motor milestones, coordination, and community wellness.

  • Hand-eye coordination & fine motor dexterity via Diketo
  • Spatial awareness, agility & cardiovascular health via Kgati & Dibeke
  • Joyful, culturally rooted pediatric neurodevelopment
