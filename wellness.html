Movement that

Movement programmes for healthier lifestyles.

Long hours at a desk, stress and busy schedules leave little room to move. Boikanya Wellness creates movement experiences and programmes for individuals, workplaces and communities that make physical activity more accessible, enjoyable and sustainable.

Programmes

Find the right way to move.

Every programme is shaped around the people taking part: their goals, their space and their time.

Workplaces

For teams and employers

Desk-bound days lead to stiff backs, low energy and avoidable strain. We bring practical movement into your workplace to support healthier work cultures and reduce injury risk.

  • On-site movement workshops and screenings
  • Ergonomics and posture strategies
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives
Request a proposal

Communities

For community groups and organisations

Not everyone has easy access to a gym or a programme. We take movement to communities, drawing on traditional African games like Diketo, Morabaraba, Kgati and Dibeke to make activity familiar and fun.

  • Culturally rooted movement sessions
  • Activities for different ages and abilities
  • Movement education for healthier habits
Read about indigenous games

Groups

For friends, clubs and small groups

Staying motivated on your own is hard. Our group classes blend mobility, strength and mindful movement in a supportive setting that builds capacity and community.

  • Small-group formats for attention and safety
  • Programming for different ages and abilities
  • Progressive, enjoyable sessions
Class enquiry

Youth

For schools, sports clubs and parents

Screens and sitting are crowding out play. We use games and playful movement to help children and teens build coordination, confidence and a love of being active.

  • Play-based movement sessions
  • Coordination, balance and agility
  • Indigenous games that celebrate culture
Bring us to your school

Events

Planning a wellness day, team-building or community event? We add movement activations and screenings that get people involved and talking about their health.

  • Wellness days and expos
  • Team-building movement sessions
  • Fitness and movement screenings
