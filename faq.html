General Questions

What is Biokinetics?
Biokinetics is a healthcare profession focused on using exercise and movement to improve health, function, and quality of life, especially for those with chronic conditions or injuries. Our biokineticists use evidence-based exercise prescription to help you achieve your movement goals.
Where is the practice located?

Appointments & Bookings

Can I reschedule or cancel?
Yes, but please provide at least 24 hours' notice to avoid a cancellation fee. You can manage your bookings through our online system or contact us directly.
Is a referral required?
No referral is needed. However, if you are under medical supervision, we can coordinate with your healthcare provider to ensure comprehensive care.
Are sessions covered by medical aid?
Yes! We accept most major medical aid schemes including Discovery, Momentum, Bonitas, and others. Coverage depends on your specific plan and benefits. We can assist with claims and provide necessary documentation. Contact your provider to verify your biokinetics benefits.
What should I bring to my appointment?
Comfortable clothing for exercise, a list of current medications, any relevant medical reports or imaging, and your medical aid card if applicable.
