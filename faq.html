Biokinetics is the science of movement and the application of scientifically prescribed exercise for rehabilitation, chronic disease management, and sports performance. While physiotherapy typically manages acute pain and provides hands-on manual manipulation immediately after injury, biokinetics takes over in the sub-acute and final phases to rebuild muscle symmetry, restore joint stability, and prevent re-injury through active movement.
