The Philosophy

What "Boikanya" Represents

In Setswana, Boikanya embodies self-reliance, trust, and resilience. It represents reclaiming control, rebuilding strength, and restoring balance in the body through intentional motion.

We believe that rehabilitation should never be a passive, clinical chore. By pairing rigorous biomechanical analysis with joyful, culturally resonant movement patterns, we help clients build physical autonomy that extends far beyond the clinic doors.

"Our brand helps clients recover from injury, manage pain, and improve functional movement, while extending care beyond the clinic through guided home programs."
Our Vision

To Empower Everyday Movement

To become a trusted movement-recovery brand that empowers people to move better, live stronger, and take ownership of their physical well-being through guided exercise and accessible recovery tools.

Our Mission

Structured, Safe, Lifelong Movement

To deliver personalized, movement-focused rehabilitation through structured workouts, expert guidance, and thoughtfully designed home recovery equipment—helping clients recover safely, build strength, and sustain healthy movement for life.

Lead Clinician

Duduetsang Keokame

Founder & Registered Biokineticist (B.Sc Hons Biokinetics)

Duduetsang is a dedicated biokineticist registered with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) and the Biokinetics Association of South Africa (BASA).

Her clinical passion centers on bridging cutting-edge orthopaedic and neurological exercise science with the cultural richness of African indigenous games. Her work empowers patients across the spectrum—from pediatric neuromuscular motor development and maternal postpartum care to elite athletic return-to-play.
