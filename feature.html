Why Choose Boikanya Biokinetics

We deliver structured, movement-based programmes tailored to your biomechanical profile, backed by university qualifications and best practices.

HPCSA & BASA Registered

Care delivered by accredited biokineticists adhering to the highest ethical and professional standards.

Personalised Programmes

Exercise prescriptions customized to your physical baseline, diagnostic findings, and lifestyle goals.

Evidence-Based Science

Grounded in modern biomechanics, mechanobiology, and exercise physiology research.

Interdisciplinary Care

Seamless collaboration with orthopaedic surgeons, physiotherapists, and general practitioners.

Modern Facility

Located at Virgin Active Eco-Park, Centurion, with state-of-the-art rehabilitation apparatus.

Objective Milestones

Standardised re-assessments every 6–8 weeks to track range of motion, power, and functional return.

Medical Aid Claimable

Direct reimbursement compatible with Discovery Health, Medscheme, Bonitas, Momentum, and Fedhealth.

Cultural Play Integration

Reviving movement through African indigenous games and physical literacy for children and groups.
