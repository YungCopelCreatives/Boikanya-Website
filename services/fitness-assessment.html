Assess, Measure, and Plan Your Training

Our fitness assessments evaluate strength, mobility, cardiovascular capacity, and functional movement to inform a personalized training or rehabilitation plan.

Common Assessment Needs

  • Unclear fitness baseline or starting point
  • Plateau in current training progress
  • Concern about movement quality
  • Need for performance optimization
  • Pre-activity screening requirements
  • Post-injury return-to-sport evaluation
  • Chronic condition exercise guidance
  • Age-related fitness changes

What We Assess

  • Cardiovascular endurance and aerobic capacity
  • Muscular strength and endurance testing
  • Flexibility and mobility range of motion
  • Body composition analysis
  • Functional movement patterns
  • Postural alignment and stability
  • Balance and coordination testing
  • Movement quality screening

How We Help

Our evidence-based fitness assessment process includes:

  • Comprehensive screening using validated protocols
  • Baseline measurement for progress tracking
  • Risk assessment for safe exercise participation
  • Goal alignment with your objectives
  • Personalized planning based on results
  • Evidence-based recommendations for training
  • Progress monitoring and re-assessment
  • Integration with healthcare when needed
